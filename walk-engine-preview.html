ConvoScape · walk engine preview

No build step, no API key. The perspective and gait model from lib/scene/perspective.ts and lib/scene/walkPath.ts, calibrated against your hall and running the real approach path. Open it from inside the project folder and it picks up hall.png and the foreground plate on its own; otherwise it falls back to a grey-box room.

Constant world speed. Depth falls at a steady rate, so screen position follows a 1/(a+bt) curve and apparent size grows the way a real approach does — slowly at first, then quickly over the last two metres.
